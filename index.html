How AgriWaste to Worth Works

From your field to the industrial furnace in three friction-free steps, guaranteed by escrow.

1

Field Scan & Grading

Take a photo of your harvested residue with your smartphone camera. Our edge AI determines the crop type, estimates moisture percentage, and calculates fair market rate per quintal or tonne.

2

Verified Mill Bidding

Your lot is broadcast to 20+ nearby power plants, paper mills, and briquette processors. Buyers place binding purchase orders backed by platform escrow holding.

3

Baler Pickup & Direct DBT

Automated tractor balers arrive at your GPS field location. Upon certified weighbridge entry at the factory gate, payment is released directly into your bank account within 24 hours.

Farmer & Buyer Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions (सामान्य प्रश्न)

Everything you need to know about stubble pricing, transport logistics, and payments

🌾 How does a farmer get paid for crop stubble?

Payment is settled through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly into the farmer's linked bank account within 24 to 48 hours of weighbridge receipt verification at the buying mill or processing center. Buyers deposit funds into an escrow holding reserve before trucks depart, ensuring zero payment default.

🚜 Who arranges the baler machinery and field transport?

Our platform coordinates with local village aggregators and Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs). When you confirm a sale, certified balers and flatbed tractor-trailers are dispatched directly to your field coordinates. Transport costs are either covered by the industrial off-taker or subsidized under the central Crop Residue Management (CRM) scheme.

💧 What if my crop residue has high moisture content?

Our AI Analyzer tool estimates moisture level before listing. If moisture exceeds 20%, the system advises 48 hours of field drying or automatically connects you with local bio-gas (CBG) digestors or vermicompost processors who actively prefer high-moisture green organic biomass.

🏭 How do industrial buyers verify biomass quality before buying?

Every listing contains lab-certified calorific values (MJ/kg), moisture readings, and verified digital weighbridge slips. Biomass plants receive digital quality certificates, GST-compliant e-Way bills, and can track delivery trucks in real-time via live logistics telematics.
