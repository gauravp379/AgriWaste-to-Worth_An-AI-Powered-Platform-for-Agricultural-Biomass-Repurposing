Major Capstone Research Project 2026

The Stubble Crisis, The Mandate, & The Circular Bio-Economy

AgriWaste to Worth is an AI-powered agricultural commodity exchange designed to dismantle the economic roots of stubble burning in India through decentralized demand aggregation, computer vision, and transparent bank settlements.

1. The Ground Reality

Why Farmers Burn: The 15-Day Trap

Every October and November, an estimated 35 million metric tonnes of rice paddy straw (parali) are set on fire across the Indo-Gangetic plains of Punjab, Haryana, and Western Uttar Pradesh. This is not reckless destruction; it is an economic bind.

Between harvesting the monsoon paddy crop and preparing seedbeds for the winter wheat sowing, farmers have a razor-thin window of only 15 to 20 days. Delays in sowing reduce wheat yields by up to 1% per day. While in-situ stubble mulching machinery (Super-SMS, Happy Seeders) exists, renting this equipment costs farmers between ₹2,800 to ₹3,500 per acre in additional diesel and operational expenses. In contrast, a single matchstick costs nothing.

2. The Structural Solution

Biomass Demand Aggregation & AI Grading

Under the Ministry of Power's revised policy, all coal-based thermal power plants across India (including NTPC) are legally mandated to co-fire 5% to 10% agricultural biomass pellets alongside coal. Furthermore, the Ministry of Petroleum's SATAT scheme aims to construct over 5,000 Compressed Bio-Gas (CBG) refineries across rural India.

The problem is not lack of demand; it is decentralized logistics and quality uncertainty. AgriWaste to Worth bridges this chasm by:

  • AI Edge Verification: Farmers scan residue in the field with their mobile phones. A trained neural network instantly predicts crop type, calorific energy ($MJ/kg$), and moisture content ($12 ext{--}15\%$) to eliminate buyer dispute.
  • Escrow Security & Baler Logistics: Platform holding funds protect the transaction. Local Custom Hiring Centres dispatch baler tractors upon order confirmation, and payment settles via DBT within 24 hours of factory gate weighbridge receipt.
  • Scope-3 Decarbonization Ledger: Every purchase automatically generates certified carbon credits ($1.45 ext{ T } CO_2e$ per tonne of paddy straw spared from open fire), auditable under BEE standards.
